City Disco · brand direction · step 1 of 3

Three ways City Disco could look and sound

One shared brief, then three concept routes shown on the same specimens: app icon, wordmark, palette, type, a quest card in light and dark, and badges. Pick one and the next step turns it into final assets for the app.

Positioning

For people who think they already know their city, and for weekend visitors who want more than a top-10 list: City Disco turns seven countries into a map you complete, one quest at a time. Unlike guidebooks it rewards doing; unlike review apps it tells you what to do next.

Personality

  • Curious: always one more street to try
  • Upbeat: celebrates small wins, never nags
  • Local: knows the waterzooi place, not the tourist trap
  • Clear: the map comes first, decoration second

Tone of voice

DoClimb the Domtoren. 465 steps, best view in Utrecht.
Don'tEmbark on an unforgettable journey to the iconic tower!
DoQuest done. +40 XP, two to go for level 7.
Don'tCongratulations!!! You are amazing!!!

Short, second person, concrete numbers and names. Excitement comes from the fact, not the exclamation marks.

Recommendation: A · Mirror Pin

It is the only route that makes the name memorable, it keeps the mint people already know, and the pin reads at every icon size from the launcher to the favicon. The spark yellow gives XP and rewards their own colour without touching the See / Eat & drink / Do colours, so the map stays readable. Bricolage Grotesque adds character in headings while Inter keeps the dense screens calm.

If you prefer something quieter, B is the safe second choice. Elements can also be mixed, for example A's icon with B's line-style XP bar.

Outside help worth getting

Name and trademark check

Search “City Disco” in the EUIPO and Benelux (BOIP) trademark registers and on the Play Store before investing in the brand publicly. Bars and events use similar names; a clash in class 9 (apps) or 41 (entertainment) is what matters. Free to search yourself; a trademark attorney if you want to register.

A designer for one polish pass (optional)

The marks here are hand-built SVG and work in the app. If City Disco goes beyond a hobby, a few hours of a freelance logo designer refining the chosen mark (optical balance, small-size version, store graphics) is the best value spend.

Fonts and icons: nothing to buy

Bricolage Grotesque, Figtree, Unbounded and Inter are all SIL Open Font License, free for apps and web. Lucide icons are ISC licensed. No AI image tool is needed: every asset is vector.
