Privacy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

City Disco is a hobby project. It collects as little as it can, shows no ads and uses no tracking or analytics.

Without an account

Everything stays on your device: your explorer profile, the quests you completed, your journal notes, saved quests and the photos you add. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing the app's data in your browser or phone removes it.

With an account (optional)

If you sign in, the following is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, in the EU) so you can use it on another device:

  • your email address and sign-in details (handled by Firebase Authentication),
  • your explorer profile, completed and saved quests and journal notes,
  • compressed copies of the photos you add to quests,
  • place suggestions, problem reports and feedback you send, with your explorer name.

Only you can read your save and your photos. Suggestions, reports and feedback are read by the person who maintains City Disco. This data is used for nothing else and is not shared or sold.

Community rankings

Once a day, the quests signed-in explorers have completed are counted into anonymous totals, such as "Done by 120 explorers" and the most explored regions. Only the totals are published, never names, accounts, dates or who did what, and a number is only shown once at least three explorers share it. Explorers without an account are not counted. To leave yourself out, turn off Explorer → Settings → Count me in the community rankings.

Your location

The app asks for your location only when you tap a button that needs it (my location, near me, check in). Your position is used on your device to show where you are and how far quests are. It is not stored and not sent to us.

Other services the app talks to

To work, the app loads data from these services, which see your IP address and the request, as with any website:

  • GitHub Pages (hosts the app),
  • OpenFreeMap (map tiles, based on OpenStreetMap),
  • Wikimedia Commons (quest photos),
  • Photon by Komoot (place search: receives the text you type in the search box),
  • the FOSSGIS OpenStreetMap router (walking routes for trails: receives the trail's stops, not your position),
  • Google Firebase (its code loads on every visit; your data goes there only when you sign in),
  • Google Maps (only when you tap Directions; it opens outside the app).

The app sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's storage to keep your progress.

Deleting your data

  • Account: Explorer → Settings → Delete my account removes your account, cloud save, cloud photos and the suggestions you sent.
  • This device: clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app.

Questions

Use Explorer → Settings → Send feedback in the app.
